# Glossary
- Provider: adapter that searches one external source.
- TorrentResult: normalized provider result.
- TorrentResolver: validates selected source and resolves metadata/file.
- Playback session: bounded server-side lifecycle for playback.
- Remux: repackage streams without re-encoding where compatible.
- Transcode: decode and re-encode media.
- HLS: playlist and media segments delivered over HTTP.
- Static provider registry: providers compiled in and enabled by config.
- ADR: Architecture Decision Record.
